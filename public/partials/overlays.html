SubMaker Guide

How SubMaker Works in Stremio & Nuvio

Translate subtitles into your language instantly with AI, enjoy automated episode prefetching with Binge Mode, and customize your subtitle experience across any video player.

⚡ Quick 3-Step Setup

  1. Add your Gemini API key (free from Google AI Studio) or enable alternative AI providers (Claude, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Grok).
  2. Pick your Source Languages (e.g. English) and Target Languages (the language you want to watch in).
  3. Click "Install in Stremio" (or copy the manifest link for Nuvio/mobile) and open any movie or series stream.

🍿 Translating in Your Player

1. Open the subtitles list in Stremio or Nuvio.
2. Select the translated track option (e.g. "Albanian - AI Translated" or "[AI Translate]").
3. The AI translates in the background while progressive chunks stream in within 15 seconds. Reselect to load the complete translated subtitles!

⚡ Predictive Binge-Watching

When watching TV shows or anime, Binge Mode automatically pre-translates and caches the next episode in the background. When you click "Next Episode", subtitles are already 100% ready with 0-second wait time!

🔄 Instant Retranslation

If a subtitle seems out of sync or you want a fresh translation, triple-click the subtitle track within 6 seconds to force an immediate re-translation and cache refresh.

🧠 Subtitle Intelligence

SubMaker automatically strips non-speech audio cues ([cheering], ♪ music ♪), balances lines at 40 CPL, localizes proper nouns, and injects character/lore context from IMDb/TMDB.

🎯 Pro Sync Tip

Because translation lists follow the same order as source subtitles, test the original subtitle for sync first (check near the beginning and middle) before translating, ensuring flawless timing.

Nuvio tip: Under Translation Settings, keep Subtitle Track Label Style set to "Language Name + AI Tag" or "Pure ISO Code" so Nuvio's language filter doesn't hide your tracks.

🧰 Sub Toolbox Instructions

🚀 How to use Sub Toolbox:

  1. In Stremio, open the subtitles list and click "Sub Toolbox".
  2. Right-click the playing stream/video in Stremio and choose "Download Subtitles" to launch the toolbox in your browser.
  3. Pick a tool: Extract & translate embedded subs, Translate subtitle files, Synchronize subtitles, or Automatic subtitles.
  4. Each tool opens in your browser using your saved SubMaker session and AI provider settings.
  5. Follow the on-screen steps, then drag the result back into Stremio if needed.
